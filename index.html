About me

I’m an experienced administrative professional with a background spanning media, nonprofit organizations, film production support, and animal healthcare environments.

After earning a BA (Hons) in Humanities from the University of Ulster, I worked in Northern Ireland supporting local government media monitoring, nonprofit communications, and training administration in the film sector — roles that required organization, discretion, and clear written communication.

Alongside strong organizational systems, I bring a personable and reassuring communication style. I’m comfortable speaking with clients, responding thoughtfully to enquiries, and helping people feel informed and at ease.

I now provide remote virtual assistant support with calm efficiency, attention to detail, and a steady, approachable presence behind the scenes.
